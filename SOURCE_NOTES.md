## Major fact sources

### NETYR identity and purpose

Source: `Signed constitution.pdf`, Article I.

- Official name: North East Texas Young Republicans.
- NETYR cultivates Republican leaders, supports responsible government,
  strengthens East Texas communities, and connects grassroots engagement with
  political leadership.

### Federation relationship

Source: `Signed constitution.pdf`, Article II.

- The governing document describes NETYR as chartered with the Texas Young
  Republican Federation and subject to its constitution and bylaws.
- No separate current charter certificate was supplied, so the public About
  page describes the broader movement relationship without asserting that
  current charter status was independently verified.

Federation context is supported by the TYRF constitution, Article II, which
describes club growth, information exchange, organizational support, leadership
training, and legislative advocacy as Federation purposes.

### Membership

Source: `Signed constitution.pdf`, Article III.

- Active Membership is available to eligible people ages 18 through 40,
  inclusive.
- Active Members must be registered voters whose primary residence is in Van
  Zandt County or an adjoining county.
- Only Active Members vote in NETYR affairs.
- The governing document sets annual dues at $30, subject to the amendment and
  special-circumstance provisions in that document.
- Associate, Contributing, and Honorary classifications are recognized.

### Leadership structure

Source: `Signed constitution.pdf`, Article IV.

- Executive Board offices: President, Vice President, Secretary, Treasurer,
  and Director of Public Relations.
- The Constitution establishes offices and responsibilities. The private
  current roster, rather than the signature page, establishes current
  officeholders and the current January 2026 – January 2028 term.

### Current officer verification

Source: `NETYR Club Roster`, private verification source.

- The roster confirms the official club name, Van Zandt County, and Canton,
  Texas identity.
- The roster confirms four named current officeholders for President, Vice
  President, Secretary, and Director of Public Relations.
- The Treasurer row has no named officeholder and therefore appears vacant.
- The roster also identifies the Director of Public Relations as Event
  Committee Chair. That secondary role is not displayed publicly.
- No member-list, address, telephone, email, birth-date, voter, or non-officer
  data was used.

### Meetings and governance

Source: `Signed constitution.pdf`, Articles V through VIII.

- Regular meetings are generally open to the general membership.
- The document contains internal governance, endorsement, financial,
  confidentiality, dissolution, and dispute provisions.
- Public pages summarize only facts needed for public understanding.

## Conflicts and ambiguities

1. **Citizenship eligibility:** The NETYR document describes membership for a
   United States citizen or a non-citizen with a pending citizenship
   application. The September 2025 TYRF constitution permits additional
   categories, including American nationals and legal permanent residents.
   NETYR is the higher-authority local source for this website, so its narrower
   language is used. Leadership should obtain legal/federation review.
2. **Article numbering:** The NETYR scan contains duplicated or inconsistent
   Roman-numeral article numbering. Public prose uses subject names rather than
   reproducing the numbering defect.
3. **Officer terminology:** The document sometimes uses both President and
   Chairman. Public pages use the explicitly listed NETYR office title
   “President.” This should be clarified in a future governing-document edit.
4. **Dues timing:** The document says annual dues are $30 and describes the
   membership period in relation to the next Executive Board installation.
   The website states the approved amount but avoids adding an unsupported
   renewal date.
5. **Geographic scope:** Active membership names Van Zandt County “or any of the
   adjoining counties.” The exact county list is not enumerated in the supplied
   NETYR document and is therefore not published.
6. **Federation status:** The governing document states that NETYR is
   chartered. No separate current TYRF charter certificate or roster was
   supplied. Public copy does not claim that current charter status was
   independently verified.
7. **Public-relations title:** The Constitution establishes "Director of Public
   Relations (Social Media)." The current roster uses "Public Relations
   Director/Event Committee Chair." The website uses the constitutional
   Executive Board title and omits the committee designation unless leadership
   later approves it for public display.
8. **Treasurer vacancy:** The Constitution requires a Treasurer office and
   describes vacancy procedures. The current roster has a Treasurer row for
   the 2026-2028 term but no current name. The website displays the position as
   vacant as approved for the production launch.
9. **Officer term wording:** The Constitution says elections occur in January
   of even-numbered years and officers serve until the next regular election.
   The roster records January 2026 – January 2028. That term is displayed for
   the four occupied offices and omitted for the vacant Treasurer office.

## Operational workbook tabs

- `Website Events` is a separate tab with the fifteen public-event
  administration fields listed in `EVENTS_INTEGRATION_GUIDE.md`.
- `Website Contacts` is a separate 15-column contact destination with a frozen
  header row. No genuine submission or roster rows were inspected. One
  non-sensitive, uniquely marked deployment test row was verified
  programmatically against the approved fields and then removed. The custom
  Apps Script package appends to this fixed tab by header name and never returns
  workbook data.
- The current header places `Message` immediately before `Preferred Contact
Method`, while the approved target structure uses the opposite order.
  `setupContactSystem()` normalizes a header-only tab; if submissions already
  exist, it preserves them and maps future values by header name instead of
  moving private data automatically.
- `Website Events` has a frozen header, filter, column sizing, date/time
  formatting, and Featured/Active checkbox validation.
- The organization-managed Events Apps Script reads only this fixed tab through
  the advanced Sheets service with a read-only OAuth scope. Its production
  endpoint passed an isolated temporary-event test, and the test row was
  removed.
- The existing roster tab was not read or changed during this event-tab
  refinement. No workbook ID is stored in the repository.

## Privacy and publication decisions

- The signed NETYR PDF includes an individual name and handwritten signature.
  It is not copied into `public/documents` pending explicit publication
  approval or preparation of an approved signature-free public copy.
- The NETYR document states that board meetings are confidential. No board
  records, internal discussions, or voting records are published.
- The TYRF documents contain rules requiring private member roster fields such
  as addresses, birth dates, phone numbers, and email addresses. None of that
  operational material is published.
- The TYRF PDFs are reference materials and are not presented as NETYR
  governing documents.
- No PDF is currently present in `public/documents/`. The signed NETYR source
  and TYRF reference documents remain outside the public build.
- The private club roster is not present in the repository, build output,
  metadata, structured data, test data, screenshots, or documentation exports.
- No approved source establishes events, sponsors, or social accounts. The
  approved public organization email is `president@netyr.org`.
