# Design Brief

## Objective

Present NETYR as a credible, active, enduring membership organization serving
young Republicans in Northeast Texas. The site should feel stately and
energetic without resembling a single-candidate campaign.

## Brand source

The supplied NETYR logo is the primary visual authority.

- Brand blue: `#0070ca`
- Brand red: `#f91d2f`
- Deep navy: `#071a33`
- Ink: `#101820`
- Silver: `#c9cdd2`
- Paper: `#f7f8fa`
- White: `#ffffff`

Blue and navy carry most of the interface. Red is reserved for calls to action,
rules, and small emphasis. Silver reflects the logo's plaque treatment.

## Typography

- Display and headings: a sturdy system sans-serif stack with compact tracking.
- Body: a highly legible system sans-serif stack.
- Small labels: uppercase with generous letter spacing.

No remote font dependency is required, improving privacy and static
performance.

## Reference video findings

The supplied video shows a desktop website for another Young Republican
chapter.

Useful direction:

- Thin, calm header with action-oriented navigation.
- Large hero statement with two clear actions.
- Alternating light and dark section rhythm.
- Strong uppercase section headings.
- Modular leadership, event, and sponsor cards.
- Restrained red buttons over deep navy backgrounds.
- Clear footer action band and social/navigation grouping.

Elements not copied:

- Organization-specific wording, people, events, policy positions, pricing,
  sponsorship amounts, or imagery.
- Exact hero treatment, accordion content, card composition, and footer layout.
- The reference site's condensed display font and proprietary visual assets.
- Its desktop-only spacing; NETYR is mobile-first.

## Component treatment

- Buttons: minimum 44-pixel target, strong focus ring, red primary and
  navy/white secondary treatments.
- Cards: white or navy surfaces with restrained borders and a small colored
  rule.
- Sections: generous but responsive vertical rhythm, never fixed-height.
- Images: natural proportions, subtle borders, no heavy overlays.
- Icons: simple inline SVG, decorative icons hidden from assistive technology.
- Motion: limited to short color and transform transitions; disabled under
  reduced-motion preferences.

## Accessibility

The design targets WCAG 2.2 AA practices:

- Semantic landmarks and headings.
- Keyboard-operable navigation.
- Visible focus states.
- Non-color status labels.
- High-contrast text and controls.
- Reduced-motion support.
- Mobile touch targets at least 44 by 44 pixels.
