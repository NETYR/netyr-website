## Control conclusion

Production source code, hosting, the live calendar, all three production Apps
Script projects, and the active spreadsheet targets are under NETYR-controlled
accounts. Institutional control cannot yet be declared complete because domain,
Workspace recovery/billing/backup administration, Cheddar Up, social accounts,
and backup administrators require authorized account-level verification.
